---
title: Search
description: One OpenSearch index over every feed, lexical and semantic, fused.
---

Every [feed](/concepts/feeds/) is a document in OpenSearch, at `OPENSEARCH_URL`. A feed is indexed
again whenever it or one of its [references](/concepts/references/) commits and whenever an
[analysis](/concepts/analysis/) of it finishes, and removed when it is destroyed. The index is a derived copy: Postgres holds the record, and
search answers with ids that are loaded back from it in the order they ranked.

## What a document holds

| Field | From | Analyzed as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tenant_id` | the feed's tenant | long |
| `type`, `mime` | the feed | keyword |
| `tags` | the keys of its tags | keyword |
| `key`, `title` | the feed | path |
| `locator_key` | its original references, joined | path |
| `keywords` | the summary's keywords and entities, across the feed and its children | path, and `keywords.raw` as keyword |
| `note` | the feed | standard |
| `summary` | every summary across the feed and its children | standard |
| `body` | everything its analyses extracted, across the feed and its children | standard |
| `resource_ids` | where its original references live | long |
| `created_at` | the feed | date |
| `embedding` | the feed's vector, when it has one | `knn_vector`, HNSW, cosine |

The `path` analyzer splits on slashes, backslashes, dashes, underscores, dots and whitespace, then
lowercases, so `invoices/2026/acme-q3.pdf` is found by `acme`. Only original references contribute
a locator or a resource id; a preview or thumbnail never does. The body leaves out the summary step, which
has its own field, and the two bookkeeping steps, `placement` and `derived`, whose results are
paths and store names rather than anything the file says. The mapping is not dynamic: a field not
listed here is not indexed.

## Lexical and semantic

A query with text is a `multi_match` with every term required, boosted `title`, `key` and
`keywords` three times, `note`, `summary` and `tags` twice, and `locator_key` and `body` once. No
text matches everything. Facets narrow either way, each taking one value or a list:

| Facet | Field |
| --- | --- |
| `type` | `type` |
| `mime` | `mime` |
| `tag` | `tags` |

Semantic search joins in when the query has text, an inference resource declares a model for the
`embedding` role, and the page asked for ends within the first 200 results. The query is embedded —
cached for a day per tenant, resource, model and text — and the top 200 lexical hits and top 200
nearest neighbours under the same facets are fused by reciprocal rank fusion: each list adds
`1 / (60 + rank)` to a feed's score, counting ranks from one, and the fused list is ordered by
score. Fusion reads only positions, never the two engines' scores, which are not on the same scale.

Past 200, or when there is no embedding resource, or when embedding the query fails, search is
lexical only. A vector query the engine refuses is logged and contributes nothing. Pages are
offsets: `from` and `limit` slice the fused list, and the reported total is the larger of the
lexical hit count and the fused length.

## Embeddings

`EmbedItemsJob` runs every minute in every tenant and embeds up to fifty feeds with no
`embedded_at`. What is embedded is a gist — title, keywords, summaries, note and the first 2,000
characters of the body, capped at 8,000 — and a digest of that gist and the model is kept beside
the vector. A feed whose digest has not moved is stamped rather than embedded again. Changing a
title or note, finishing an analysis, or committing a reference clears the stamp; changing the
embedding model clears every stamp in the tenant, because two models do not share a vector space.
The vector's dimension is `URIS_EMBEDDING_DIMENSIONS`, 768 by default.

## Tenants

Every tenant's documents share one index. Each tenant searches through its own alias, created when
the tenant is, which carries a filter on `tenant_id`, and the nearest-neighbour query repeats that
filter inside itself. A search with no tenant is refused.

## Rebuilding

The index's settings and mapping are hashed into a stamp stored in the index's metadata.
`RebuildSearchIndexJob` runs daily at 3am and does nothing unless the live stamp differs. When it
does, it builds a new versioned index, reindexes every tenant into it in pages of 200 under a run
of kind `reindex`, and refuses to promote it holding fewer documents than there are feeds. Promotion
moves the main alias and every tenant alias in one request and deletes the old index; a failure
deletes the new one and leaves the old one serving. Feeds that changed during the rebuild are
indexed again afterwards.
