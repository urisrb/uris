uris catalogs what you already have. It points at the places your things live — buckets,
directories, mailboxes, calendars, feeds, wikis, repositories — reads each one, and gives you one
index over all of them that you can search, ask questions of, and export back out. The bytes stay
where they are; uris keeps where they live and what a pass over them understood.

Two interfaces reach the same catalog: a browser app over [GraphQL](/reference/graphql/), and an
[MCP endpoint](/concepts/agents/) a model can drive. Both take the same token, issued by
[masks](https://github.com/urisrb/masks), and neither wraps the other.
